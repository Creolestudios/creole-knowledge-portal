# 📰 User Manual: Blog & Daily Learning Portal
**Audience:** Employees & Portal Users  
**Module:** Daily Tech Blog Fetch, Synthesis & Verified Knowledge Quiz

---

## 1. Overview & Navigation
When you log into the knowledge portal, look at the dark sidebar navigation on the left. You will see 5 primary sections:
* **Daily Blog**
* **Past Blogs**
* **Activity Tracker**
* **Leaderboard**
* **Blog Roulette**

---

## 2. Reading the Daily Blog

### 1️⃣ "Daily Blog" Tab (Your Morning Tech Read)
This is your main dashboard.

* **Scenario A: Today’s blog is already generated**
  1. You will see today’s blog title, technical tags, and an executive summary.
  2. As you scroll and read, a **live reading timer** in the corner automatically tracks your reading time towards your daily 10-minute goal.
  3. At the top right, click **"Read Original Source"** if you want to view the full original external article.
  4. Once you finish reading, scroll to the bottom and click **"Take Today's Quiz"** to enter the quiz runner.

* **Scenario B: Today’s blog has not arrived yet**
  1. You will see a clean empty state card stating that today's briefing is not generated yet.
  2. You will see a prominent button: **"Synthesize Today's Briefing"**.
  3. **Click "Synthesize Today's Briefing"**:
     - A progress checklist appears on screen as the system gathers top engineering blogs and writes your custom summary.
     - Within a few seconds, today’s new blog digest loads automatically on your screen, ready to read.

---

## 3. Taking the Daily Quiz (Permissions, Questions & Answers)

When you click **"Take Today's Quiz"**, the interactive Quiz Runner opens. To ensure honest learning, the quiz includes a quick verification setup before displaying the questions.

### Step 1: Equipment & Proctoring Permissions
Before questions appear, you must complete the 2 required permission steps:
1. **Screen Share Permission**:
   - The browser prompts you to share your screen.
   - Select **"Entire Screen"** and click **Share** (this ensures you remain on the quiz window).
2. **Camera Access Permission**:
   - The browser prompts you to allow webcam access.
   - Click **"Allow"**. A small live camera preview window appears in the corner to confirm you are seated in front of your screen.
3. **Review Instructions**:
   - A brief rules card confirms: 5 questions total, a countdown timer, and a reminder that **switching tabs or leaving the screen will automatically submit the quiz immediately**.
   - Click **"Start Quiz"**.

### Step 2: Answering Quiz Questions
1. **Question Types**:
   - **Multiple Choice**: Read the question and click on the best answer option (A, B, C, or D).
   - **Conceptual / Code Questions**: Type your brief explanation or code answer directly into the text box.
2. **Progress & Navigation**:
   - At the top, you can see your current question counter (e.g., *"Question 2 of 5"*).
   - A live timer displays elapsed time.
   - Click **"Next Question"** to proceed, or **"Previous Question"** to review your past answers.
   - The small camera preview stays active in the corner while you answer.

### Step 3: Submitting & Reviewing Results
1. On the 5th and final question, click the green **"Submit Quiz"** button.
2. **Instant Scorecard**:
   - Your final score is shown immediately (e.g., *4/5 — 80%*).
   - You will see a clear **Passed** or **Needs Improvement** badge.
   - A breakdown shows which questions you answered correctly, along with explanations for any questions you missed.
3. **Attempt Policy**: You have up to 3 attempts per daily blog quiz to improve your score.
4. Click **"Back to Dashboard"** — your quiz score and reading time are automatically recorded on your Activity Tracker and Leaderboard!

## 4. Other Learning Features

### 2️⃣ "Past Blogs" Tab (Missed a Day?)
* **How to use it:**
  1. Click **"Past Blogs"** in the sidebar.
  2. You will see an interactive **calendar** and a chronological list of previous dates.
  3. Click on any past date (for example, *"Yesterday"* or *"Last Friday"*).
  4. **What happens:** The portal instantly displays the exact blog digest, summary, and quiz from that selected day.

---

### 3️⃣ "Activity Tracker" Tab (Your Personal Learning Stats)
* **How to use it:**
  1. Click **"Activity Tracker"** in the sidebar.
  2. **What you see on screen:**
     - **🔥 Reading Streak**: Shows how many consecutive days you have completed your daily reading (e.g., *7-Day Streak!*).
     - **Weekly Minutes Bar Chart**: Shows total minutes spent reading from Monday to Friday.
     - **Quiz Performance**: Displays your average quiz accuracy score and total quizzes completed.

---

### 4️⃣ "Leaderboard" Tab (Team Standings)
* **How to use it:**
  1. Click **"Leaderboard"** in the sidebar.
  2. **What you see on screen:**
     - A ranked list of all colleagues across the company.
     - Rankings are based on total learning points, quiz correctness, and active streaks.
