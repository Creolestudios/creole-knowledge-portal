# AI Interview Question Bank

This document lists the reusable questions currently available for administrator selection. The admin can select one or more categories and then choose the exact questions to include in an interview.

## Categories

- HR
- Behavioral
- Experience Overview
- Role Alignment
- Project Experience
- Teamwork
- Adaptability
- Conflict Resolution
- Work Preferences
- Career Vision
- Culture Fit

## HR

1. Please introduce yourself and highlight the experiences most relevant to this role.
2. What are the most important factors you consider when evaluating a new opportunity?
3. What type of support helps you perform well when starting a new role?
4. What is your notice period and when would you be available to start?

## Behavioral

1. Tell us about a challenging professional situation and what you learned from handling it.
2. Describe a time you received difficult feedback and how you responded.
3. Tell us about a decision you made with incomplete information.
4. Describe a professional mistake and the steps you took afterward.

## Experience Overview

1. Walk us through your career journey and the choices that shaped it.
2. Which previous responsibility best prepared you for this position?
3. What achievement from your previous work best represents your capabilities?
4. Which part of your background would you like us to understand better?

## Role Alignment

1. What interests you most about this role and its responsibilities?
2. Which requirement of this role matches your strongest experience?
3. Which aspect of this role would require the most preparation from you?
4. How would you define success in this role during your first few months?

## Project Experience

1. Tell us about a project you are proud of and your specific contribution.
2. What was the biggest challenge in a recent project, and how did you address it?
3. How did you decide what to prioritize during a project?
4. What would you do differently if you repeated one of your previous projects?

## Teamwork

1. Describe a time you worked with people who had different working styles.
2. How do you keep teammates informed when your work affects theirs?
3. What role do you naturally take when working in a team?
4. Tell us about a time your team achieved a result together.

## Adaptability

1. How do you approach learning an unfamiliar tool, process, or domain?
2. Tell us about a time priorities changed unexpectedly.
3. How do you maintain quality when you must change direction quickly?
4. What is an example of a process or habit you changed after learning something new?

## Conflict Resolution

1. Tell us about a disagreement with a teammate and how you resolved it.
2. How would you respond if you disagreed with an important decision?
3. Describe a time you helped two people reach an agreement.
4. What do you do when a disagreement starts affecting team progress?

## Work Preferences

1. What type of work environment helps you perform at your best?
2. How do you organize your work when several tasks are competing for attention?
3. How do you prefer to receive direction and feedback from a manager?
4. How do you maintain focus during repetitive or routine work?

## Career Vision

1. What professional skills would you like to develop over the next few years?
2. How does this role fit into your longer-term career direction?
3. What kind of responsibility would you like to take on next?
4. What would make your next career step successful?

## Culture Fit

1. What team values are most important to you?
2. How do you contribute to a respectful and positive team environment?
3. How do you work effectively with people whose perspectives differ from yours?
4. What does professional integrity mean in everyday work?

## Selection Behavior

- Each category currently contains 4 available questions.
- The administrator selects categories first.
- Questions for selected categories are then displayed individually.
- The administrator selects the exact questions to ask.
- The configured total must equal the number of selected questions.
- The selected questions are sent to the interview-question generation flow.
- Database persistence is not included in this phase.
