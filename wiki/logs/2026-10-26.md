# 2026-10-26 — Dynamic Interview Instructions for Warning Bypass

## Overview
Added dynamic instruction and rule card filtering on the interview preparation screen (`ProctoringInstructions`). When an interview is initiated with Warning Bypass enabled, 3-warning termination policy and 30-second auto-resume guidelines are omitted to accurately match the bypass session behavior.

## Details
- **Component Updated**: `components/ai-interview/proctoring-instructions.tsx`
  - Added `isBypass?: boolean` prop (defaults to `false`).
  - Created `BYPASS_INSTRUCTIONS` omitting strict 3-warning termination and auto-resume items.
  - Created `BYPASS_DETECTION_RULES` omitting the 3-Warning Termination Policy card.
  - Dynamic status badge showing `Warning Bypass Enabled` when in bypass mode vs `Active AI Proctoring` in standard mode.
- **Pages Updated**:
  - `app/interview/[id]/page.tsx`: Passes `isBypass={bypassProctoring}` to `<ProctoringInstructions>`.
  - `app/assess/[token]/page.tsx`: Retrieves `bypassProctoring` from verification and passes `isBypass={bypassProctoring}` to `<ProctoringInstructions>`.
  - `app/api/assess/[token]/verify/route.ts`: Exposes `bypassProctoring` boolean based on invite `max_alerts > 1000` or `parsed_jd.bypassProctoring`.
- **Testing**:
  - `tests/ai-interview-warning-policy.test.tsx`: Added unit test verifying that 3-warning termination and auto-resume rules are hidden when `isBypass=true`.
