# 🎓 Candidate Manual: AI Interview Module
**Audience:** Job Applicants & Interviewees  
**Module:** Live AI Candidate Assessment & Proctoring Journey

---

## Step 1: Accessing the Assessment
1. Open the interview link provided to you.
2. Enter your **Email Address**.
3. Enter your **Passcode**.
4. Click **"Continue"**.

---

## Step 2: Mandatory Proctoring Instructions Screen
Before any questions or timers begin, you will see the **Proctoring Instructions Screen** detailing all test integrity rules:
* ⚠️ Do not switch tabs, minimize the browser, or open other applications — doing so triggers immediate termination with no option to resume.
* ⚠️ Keep your face centered and visible to the camera at all times — leaving the camera view flags proctoring violations.
* ⚠️ Strictly no secondary devices — mobile phones, smartwatches, or physical notes are strictly prohibited. *(Note: Earphones or headphones are only permitted if you are using a connected Bluetooth headset or headphones for the interview; otherwise, they are not allowed).*
* ⚠️ Sit in a quiet room — background voices, secondary conversations, or external audio will be flagged in your evaluation report.

Read and confirm these instructions before proceeding to the setup step.

---

## Step 3: Equipment, Audio & Screen Permissions Check
1. The system asks for full permissions to establish a verified assessment environment:
   - **Camera Access**: Allows webcam video.
   - **Microphone Access**: Allows recording your spoken answers.
   - **Screen-Share & System Audio Access**: When prompted by your browser to share your screen, select your entire screen and **ensure the "Also share system audio" box is checked** (this confirms no external voice or internal media are playing on your machine).
2. Click **"Allow"** and select your entire screen.
3. A live camera preview box appears to verify your lighting and microphone sound levels.
4. Complete the quick 3-second face calibration to confirm your face is centered in the circle.
5. Click **"Start Interview"**.

---

## Step 4: During the Interview
1. The AI interviewer introduces itself, reads the question aloud, and displays the question text on screen.
2. A timer displays the countdown remaining for that specific question.
3. Speak your answer clearly into your microphone in your own natural voice.
4. Once you have finished speaking, click **"Next Question"** (if the timer runs out before clicking, the interview will automatically advance).
5. ⛔ **Immediate Termination on Tab Switching**: If you click out of the interview tab, switch to another browser tab, or minimize the window, the interview is cut off immediately and displays the **"Interview Terminated"** screen. You cannot re-enter or resume the session.

---

## Step 5: Completing the Interview
1. After submitting your answer to the final question, the interview wraps up automatically.
2. A green confirmation screen appears: **"Interview Completed — Thank You!"**
3. Once the upload progress bar reaches 100% completion, your interview is successfully submitted and you can safely close your browser window.
