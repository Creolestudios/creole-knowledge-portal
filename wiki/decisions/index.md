# ⚖️ Architectural Decisions

This section documents the key technical choices that shape the Creole Knowledge Portal, recorded as Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

## 📚 Decision Log

- [[wiki/decisions/001-app-router|ADR-001: Use Next.js App Router]] - Choice of routing paradigm for the frontend.

---
**ADR Format:**
- **Status**: Accepted / Proposed / Superseded
- **Context**: Why was this decision necessary?
- **Decision**: What was chosen?
- **Consequences**: What are the trade-offs and impacts?
